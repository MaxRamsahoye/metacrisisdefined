    The Metacrisis

    The Metacrisis is a conceptual frame for capturing civilisation’s total problem complex, the generative dynamics that (re)produce it and the attractor states it vectors towards. It marks the terminal phase in the developmental process of the current world-system, which is now emergently posing an existential risk to humanity.
